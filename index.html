About

Probing the quantum vacuum from ultracold atom rings to quantum black holes.

I am a theoretical and mathematical physicist (Ph.D. in Physics, University of Udine, 2025; M.Sc. in Theoretical Physics 110/110 cum laude, University of Trieste, 2019). My research explores quantum field theory in curved spacetime and non-relativistic nonlinear systems—focusing on the Casimir effect, the Unruh effect and spontaneous symmetry breaking, and scalar vacuum polarization ⟨φ²⟩ around Loop Quantum Gravity black holes.

Over the past years, I have collaborated internationally—including a research stay with Prof. Antonino Flachi's group at the Research and Education Center for Natural Sciences, Keio University (Yokohama, Japan), and within INFN (Sezione di Trieste). I combine analytical tools (covariant point-splitting, ζ-function and Mellin–Barnes regularization, WKB asymptotics, and Bessel/Jacobi elliptic functions) with high-performance numerical algorithms in Python, Mathematica, and Fortran.
